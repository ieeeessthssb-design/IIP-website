## 🎓 Design Decisions Explained

### 1. Electrical Grid Metaphor
Transformed CS website's neural network into electrical energy network:
- Particles = Electrical nodes/substations
- Connections = Transmission lines
- Pulses = Energy flow
- Mouse = Electrical disturbance

### 2. Electric Green Palette
Represents:
- Electrical energy and power
- Industry and manufacturing
- Sustainability
- Technical precision

### 3. Section Numbering
Technical documentation style:
- [01] // System
- [02] // Three Pillars
- Similar to engineering schematics

### 4. Premium Dark Aesthetic
- Matches CS website DNA
- Reduces eye strain
- Green pops on dark background
- Technical terminology throughout

### 5. Data-Driven Architecture
- Events in JSON manifest
- Easy for non-technical updates
- Separation of content/presentation
- Scales to future enhancements
