[01] // About PES

Electrifying the world.

The IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES) is the world's largest professional association dedicated to advancing the electrical power industry through research, innovation, and collaboration.

We develop the engineers who design, build, and operate the electrical infrastructure that powers civilization—from generation and transmission to distribution and smart grids that enable sustainable energy futures.

[03] // Areas of Expertise

Technical disciplines

Power Generation

Conventional and renewable energy resources, generator design, and sustainable electricity production.

Transmission Systems

High-voltage networks, FACTS devices, transmission line design, and bulk power movement.

Distribution Networks

Local power delivery, voltage regulation, network optimization, and reliability engineering.

Smart Grids

Advanced metering, IoT integration, grid modernization, and demand-side management.

Renewable Energy

Solar, wind, hydro, geothermal systems integration and sustainable power solutions.

Energy Storage

Battery systems, pumped hydro, compressed air, thermal storage, and grid stabilization.

Power Electronics

Converters, inverters, power quality, and advanced semiconductor applications in grids.

Grid Resilience

Fault analysis, protection systems, outage prevention, and disaster recovery strategies.

Electrification

Vehicle charging infrastructure, industrial electrification, and sector coupling technologies.

Power Systems Planning

Load forecasting, network expansion, infrastructure investment, and policy development.
