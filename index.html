[01] // The System

One ecosystem. Three disciplines.

The IEEE ESSTHS Joint Chapter for Industrial Applications, Industrial Electronics, and Power & Energy unites three complementary technical societies under one engineering ecosystem. We connect students across the spectrum of industrial systems—from fundamental power generation and transmission to advanced automation, control systems, and intelligent industrial applications.

Through collaborative workshops, technical seminars, industrial visits, and hands-on projects, we develop the engineers who will design, build, and optimize the industrial infrastructure of tomorrow—smarter, cleaner, and more resilient.
