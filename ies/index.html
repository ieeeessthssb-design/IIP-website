[01] // About IES

The intelligence layer of modern industry.

The IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (IES) is a technical community dedicated to advancing the theory, design, and application of industrial electronics, automation, control systems, robotics, and intelligent systems that optimize industrial processes and enable human-machine collaboration.

Through workshops, technical seminars, research talks, and hands-on projects, we develop engineers who understand how sensing, computation, control, electronics, and physical systems work together.
