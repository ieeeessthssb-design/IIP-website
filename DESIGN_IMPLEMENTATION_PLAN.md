## 🏭 IAS Identity (NEW DESIGN)

### Brand Definition
**Industry in Motion** - A modern industrial facility control environment

### Color Palette

#### Primary Color
- **IEEE IAS Green**: #00843D
- **Usage**: Main accent, titles, hero elements
- **Reason**: Official IEEE IAS color (formal, industrial)
- **Psychology**: Authority, reliability, industrial heritage

#### Secondary Color
- **Copper**: #B87333
- **Usage**: Highlights, machinery elements, technical accents
- **Reason**: Represents electrical conductivity, metal/mechanical
- **Psychology**: Warmth, mechanical, industrial equipment

#### Accent Color
- **Industrial Gold**: #D9A441
- **Usage**: Status indicators, energy highlights, CTAs
- **Reason**: Represents efficiency, industrial excellence, premium quality
- **Psychology**: Quality, precision, manufactured goods

#### Supporting Colors
- **Industrial Black**: #0A0E0C (vs. #050806)
- **Industrial Surface**: #0F1612 (vs. #0B120D) - slightly warmer
- **Steel Gray**: #6B7A73 (vs. #7A8A7D) - warmer gray
- **Off-White**: #F5F5F5 (same)

### Particle System Design
**"Industrial Energy Network"** - Factory floor inspired

**Concept**: Represents power distribution, machinery nodes, motor control points

**Visual Characteristics**:
- Larger particle nodes (machinery/motor points)
- Thicker connections (power transmission lines)
- Color: Copper (#B87333) with gold pulses (#D9A441)
- Glow: Industrial amber/copper glow (not bright green)
- Movement: Mechanical, less flowy than IES
- Pulses: Power distribution (industrial power flowing through system)
- Grid Background: Factory floor grid pattern (more rectilinear)

**Particle Behavior**:
- Nodes represent: Motors, drives, power distribution points
- Connections: Direct power transmission paths
- Pulses: Energy/power flowing from source to machinery
- Mouse: Acts like load/demand spike in system

### Background Graphics
- Industrial machinery patterns
- Motor/drive schematic lines
- Factory floor grid (mechanical)
- Gauge/meter markings
- Power distribution symbology
- Valve/junction patterns

### Typography & Styling
- Same fonts as IES/IIP (Professional)
- Titles: IAS Green (#00843D)
- Accents: Copper (#B87333)
- Energy pulses: Industrial Gold (#D9A441)
- Monospace labels: Copper or Steel Gray

### Cards & Containers
- **Border**: Copper with thin gold glow on hover
- **Background**: Industrial Black (#0A0E0C)
- **Text**: Off-white with copper accents
- **Hover**: Copper border + subtle gold pulse
- **Active**: Gold highlight + copper glow

### Interactive Elements
- **Buttons**: Copper border, gold text, gold glow on hover
- **Links**: Copper text, copper underline
- **Focus States**: Gold outline
- **Loading**: Gold pulsing animation

### Visual Differentiators from IES
- ✅ Warm color palette (copper/gold) vs. cool electric green
- ✅ Mechanical/engineering patterns vs. circuit patterns
- ✅ Larger, more robust elements vs. delicate nodes
- ✅ Thicker lines/borders vs. thin connections
- ✅ Power/machinery metaphor vs. electronics metaphor
- ✅ Industrial facility aesthetic vs. digital lab aesthetic
- ✅ Amber glows vs. green glows

## ⚡ PES Identity (NEW DESIGN)

### Brand Definition
**Power & Energy Infrastructure** - An intelligent grid control center

### Color Palette

#### Primary Color
- **Infrastructure Green**: #00A86B
- **Usage**: Main structural elements, grid topology
- **Reason**: Distinct from IAS (#00843D) and IES (#39FF88)
- **Psychology**: Connected, infrastructure, natural energy

#### Primary Accent
- **Electric Cyan**: #39FF88 (shared with IES)
- **Usage**: Energy pulses, active connections, live power flow
- **Reason**: Represents electricity, energy in motion
- **Psychology**: Power, flow, intelligence

#### Grid Accent
- **Grid Teal**: #6EE7B7
- **Usage**: Transmission lines, grid overlay, secondary accents
- **Reason**: Bridges infrastructure green and electric accent
- **Psychology**: Connected systems, intelligent infrastructure

#### Supporting Colors
- **Grid Black**: #050806 (same as others)
- **Grid Surface**: #0B1612 (slightly warmer than standard)
- **Grid Gray**: #7A8A7D (cool gray for technical labels)
- **Off-White**: #F5F5F5 (same)

### Particle System Design
**"Smart Grid Network"** - Living energy infrastructure

**Concept**: Represents transmission system, substations, energy flow at grid scale

**Visual Characteristics**:
- Medium particle nodes (substations/transmission points)
- Flowing connections (transmission pathways)
- Colors: Infrastructure Green (#00A86B) nodes + Cyan (#39FF88) pulses
- Glow: Cyan energy glow (active power flow)
- Movement: Flowing, intelligent, less random than IES
- Pulses: Energy propagation through grid
- Grid Background: Smart grid topology pattern (interconnected)

**Particle Behavior**:
- Nodes represent: Substations, transmission points, load centers
- Connections: High-voltage transmission lines
- Pulses: Energy flowing from generation to consumption
- Mouse: Acts like demand surge or grid disturbance
- Multiple pulses: Simultaneous energy flows (grid complexity)

### Background Graphics
- Transmission line patterns
- Substation topology
- Grid interconnection maps
- Energy flow directions
- Power distribution hierarchies
- Smart meter patterns
- Voltage level indicators

### Typography & Styling
- Same fonts as IES/IAS (Professional)
- Titles: Infrastructure Green (#00A86B)
- Accents: Cyan (#39FF88)
- Secondary: Grid Teal (#6EE7B7)
- Monospace labels: Cyan or Grid Teal

### Cards & Containers
- **Border**: Infrastructure Green with cyan glow on hover
- **Background**: #0B1612 (slightly warmer surface)
- **Text**: Off-white with cyan accents
- **Hover**: Cyan border + active cyan pulse
- **Active**: Infrastructure Green highlight + cyan flow

### Interactive Elements
- **Buttons**: Cyan border, cyan text, infrastructure green glow on hover
- **Links**: Cyan text, cyan underline
- **Focus States**: Infrastructure Green outline
- **Loading**: Cyan flowing animation (represents energy flow)

### Visual Differentiators from IAS & IES
- ✅ Cool infrastructure palette vs. warm industrial OR pure digital
- ✅ Transmission/grid patterns vs. machinery OR circuit patterns
- ✅ Multi-level topology vs. simple factory OR neural network
- ✅ Energy flow emphasis vs. mechanical OR logical
- ✅ Grid control center aesthetic vs. factory floor OR digital lab
- ✅ Interconnected nodes vs. machinery points OR circuit elements
- ✅ Cyan pulses for power flow vs. gold machinery OR green circuits

```
                IES              IAS              PES
                ───              ───              ───

Primary:        #39FF88          #00843D          #00A86B
                Electric Green   Industrial Green Infrastructure Green

Secondary:      #16A34A          #B87333          #39FF88
                Dark Green       Copper           Electric (accent)

Accent:         #86EFAC          #D9A441          #6EE7B7
                Bright Green     Industrial Gold  Grid Teal

                Black            Industrial Black Black

                Dark Green-Black Warm Industrial  Warm Grid

                #39FF88 → #86EFAC #B87333 → #D9A441 #39FF88 → #6EE7B7
```

### Key Distinctions
1. **Color Temperature**: IES (cool digital) < PES (cool infrastructure) < IAS (warm industrial)
2. **Complexity**: IAS (machinery, direct) → PES (infrastructure, interconnected) → IES (circuit, abstract)
3. **Primary Colors**: All unique, instantly recognizable
4. **Glow Profiles**: IAS warm (copper/gold), PES cool (cyan), IES cool (green)

## 📐 Design System Implementation Strategy

### Phase 1: Color System (CSS Variables)
Update or create separate color schemes:

```css
/* Shared */
:root {
  --void: #050806;
  --white: #F5F5F5;
  --font-sans: "Plus Jakarta Sans", sans-serif;
  --font-mono: "JetBrains Mono", monospace;
}

/* IES (existing - updated) */
:root.theme-ies {
  --primary: #39FF88;
  --primary-dim: #16A34A;
  --primary-glow: #86EFAC;
  --surface: #0B120D;
  --steel: #7A8A7D;
}

/* IAS (new) */
:root.theme-ias {
  --primary: #00843D;
  --secondary: #B87333;
  --accent: #D9A441;
  --surface: #0F1612;
  --steel: #6B7A73;
}

/* PES (new) */
:root.theme-pes {
  --primary: #00A86B;
  --secondary: #39FF88;
  --accent: #6EE7B7;
  --surface: #0B1612;
  --steel: #7A8A7D;
}
```

### Phase 2: Component Customization
Adjust key components per theme:

- **Hero Particle System**: Different parameters (node size, pulse color, connection style)
- **Background Patterns**: Theme-specific grid/machinery/transmission lines
- **Card Styling**: Color schemes, borders, glows
- **Typography Colors**: Primary, secondary, accent usage
- **Interactive States**: Hover, focus, active with theme colors

### Phase 3: Page-Specific Assets
Each page needs:
- Custom SVG logo (IAS or PES branded)
- Theme-specific hero background
- Icon sets matching aesthetic
- Event images with theme overlay

## 🎭 Visual Language Comparison

### Industry (IAS)
**Environment**: Manufacturing floor with control room  
**Key Objects**: Motors, drives, machinery, power panels  
**Movement**: Mechanical, precise, load-bearing  
**Geometry**: Orthogonal, symmetrical, engineered  
**Texture**: Matte surfaces, metal, industrial  
**Sound**: Machinery hum, electrical buzz  
**Mood**: Robust, reliable, industrial heritage  

### Energy (PES)
**Environment**: Grid control center with transmission room  
**Key Objects**: Substations, transmission towers, transformers  
**Movement**: Flowing, distributed, bidirectional  
**Geometry**: Hierarchical networks, interconnected  
**Texture**: Smooth surfaces, conductive, infrastructure  
**Sound**: Electrical hum, flowing power, grid pulses  
**Mood**: Intelligent, connected, infrastructure-scale  

### Electronics (IES)
**Environment**: Digital laboratory / chip fabrication  
**Key Objects**: Circuits, logic gates, processors  
**Movement**: Organic, neural-inspired, distributed  
**Geometry**: Complex patterns, layered, abstract  
**Texture**: Digital, glowing, ethereal  
**Sound**: Digital chimes, processing, data flow  
**Mood**: Cutting-edge, intelligent, automated

## 📐 Technical Specifications

### Color Contrast Requirements (WCAG AA)
- All text vs. background: 4.5:1 minimum
- UI components vs. background: 3:1 minimum
- Color alone not used to communicate information

### Performance Targets
- Hero particle system: 60 FPS on 60Hz displays
- Page load: < 3 seconds on 4G
- First contentful paint: < 1.5 seconds

### Browser Support
- Chrome/Edge 90+
- Firefox 88+
- Safari 14+
- Mobile browsers (iOS Safari 14+, Chrome Mobile 90+)
