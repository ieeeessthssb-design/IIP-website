[01] // About IAS

Industry in motion.

The IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) is a technical community dedicated to advancing the theory, design, practice, and application of electrical systems in industrial environments. We focus on the engineering excellence that powers modern manufacturing, automation, and industrial infrastructure.

Through workshops, technical seminars, industrial visits, and hands-on projects, we develop the engineers who design, optimize, and maintain the industrial systems that drive global commerce and innovation.

[02] // Industry Applications

Core focus areas

Industrial Power Systems

Electrical generation, distribution, and utilization in industrial environments including motors, drives, and power conditioning.

Motor Drives & Control

Advanced motor control systems, variable frequency drives (VFDs), and motor efficiency optimization in industrial applications.

Manufacturing Systems

Electrical systems for industrial manufacturing, including process automation, machinery control, and factory infrastructure.

Industrial Automation

Control systems, PLCs, HMIs, and integrated automation solutions for industrial processes and production facilities.

Electrical Safety

Industrial electrical safety standards, hazard analysis, equipment protection, and compliance in manufacturing environments.
